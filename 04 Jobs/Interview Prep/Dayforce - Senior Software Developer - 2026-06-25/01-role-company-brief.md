## Candidate Positioning Angles

- Senior .NET engineer who improves systems and delivery, not just feature tickets.
- Strong modernization story: monolith decomposition, service boundaries, CI/CD, integration testing, versioning, feature flags, and local developer experience.
- Practical quality mindset: faster feedback loops, trunk-based development, testing, pipeline optimization, and production release support.
- AI angle is unusually strong for this JD: you have already built an automated workflow using Azure DevOps REST APIs, structured LLM prompts, and a background CLI tool.
- Frontend angle should be framed as "credible full-stack exposure and active learning" rather than pretending your commercial centre of gravity is frontend-heavy.

## Risks, Sensitivities, And Unknowns

- Dayforce may ask short SQL/TSQL problems. Your CV lists MySQL/Azure SQL/EF Core more clearly than TSQL specifics, so refresh SQL Server-style syntax and query plans.
- Frontend may be tested in JSFiddle. Practise plain JavaScript, DOM manipulation, arrays, events, fetch, JSON, and CSS basics.
- The role is payroll/tax. Prepare to talk about correctness, auditing, regression risk, careful migrations, and edge cases.
- The line-manager conversation may probe career direction. Have a crisp answer around remaining hands-on while growing technical leadership and mentoring.
- The panel may ask whether AI threatens quality. Frame AI as a tool under review, tests, governance, and human accountability.
