# Audit Checklist

## Signs of Thin or Stale Coverage
- Notes with `status: Needs Review` that touch fast-moving technologies.
- Notes that only contain a TL;DR and no deeper guidance despite being core topics.
- Version-sensitive topics with no external verification path or freshness note.

## Signs of Structural Weakness
- Notes with no meaningful related-concept wikilinks.
- Overlapping notes that split the same topic without a clear boundary.
- Topics that clearly belong in an existing area but live in ad hoc locations.
- Indexes that omit directly published notes or fail to link content-bearing child branches.
- Categories whose names or boundaries overlap without an explanation or cross-link.
- Treat approved planned categories as valid even when empty; emptiness alone does not justify pruning.

## Prioritization Heuristics
- Prioritize notes that support common questions or recurring decisions.
- Prioritize stale notes that could mislead readers.
- Prefer incremental expansion of nearby notes over wide taxonomy churn.
