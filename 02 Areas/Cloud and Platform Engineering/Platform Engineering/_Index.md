# Platform Engineering

## Quick Reference

Use this category for internal developer platforms, self-service infrastructure, and service-to-service platform capabilities such as a mesh.

## Published Notes

- [[Service Mesh|Service Mesh]] - A service mesh moves service-to-service networking concerns such as traffic policy, mutual TLS, retries, telemetry, and routing into a dedicated infrastructure layer, often through sidecars or node-level proxies.
