## 🎯 TL;DR / Quick Reference

**Definition:** A pool of worker threads used to execute short-lived tasks without the cost of creating threads per task.

**When to use:**
- Scheduling background work, parallel processing of independent tasks.

**Key Takeaways:**
- ✅ **Reduces thread creation overhead** and improves throughput for many small tasks.
