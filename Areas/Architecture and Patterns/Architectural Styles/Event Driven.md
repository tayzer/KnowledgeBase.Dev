# Event-Driven Architecture
Date: 2025-11-25
Status: 🟢 Current
Tags: #architecture #event-driven #messaging

## 🎯 TL;DR / Quick Reference

**Definition:** Architecture where services communicate via events, enabling decoupling and asynchronous workflows.

**When to use:**
- Systems needing high decoupling, asynchronous processing, or event-sourced patterns.

**Key Takeaways:**
- ✅ **Loose coupling** and improved scalability.
- ⚠️ **Eventual consistency** and operational complexity.
