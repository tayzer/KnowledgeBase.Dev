## 🎯 TL;DR / Quick Reference

**Definition:** A powerful, open-source object-relational database system known for standards compliance and extensibility.

**When to use:**
- Feature-rich relational workloads, GIS (PostGIS), and complex queries.

**Key Takeaways:**
- ✅ **Rich feature set** (window functions, JSONB, extensions).
- ⚡ **Extensible**: custom types, functions, and indexing.
